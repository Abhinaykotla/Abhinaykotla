## 🚀 At Anthum.ai

Anthum is an AI ad-creation platform: creators make video, image and audio with frontier models, and brands pay them on contests and attributed Shopify sales. Since February 2026 I've merged 283 PRs and written 57% of all human commits.

- 🤖 Built the AI agent from scratch, then rebuilt it to generate images, video and audio with budget and approval gates
- 💳 Built subscriptions, billing and creator payouts from scratch on Stripe and Shopify, including revenue share with order attribution, clawbacks and fraud holds
- 🛍️ Took the Shopify app through App Store submission and integrated Meta Ads to publish approved creator videos as ads
- 🎬 Integrated about 30 generative models (Veo 3.1, Sora 2, Kling, Seedance, FLUX, ElevenLabs) across six providers behind one model registry; an async pipeline cut Kling submit time from up to 48s to 610ms
- 🔒 Closed 66 publicly writable database functions, cut anonymously callable functions from 242 to 12 and re-scoped 296 Row-Level Security policies, with CI checks to block regressions
- ⚡ Reworked a health check that used 48.6% of production database time and cut 1.2 MB (gzipped) from every creator page
- 🧪 Shipped with Claude Code and Codex, backed by about 7,450 unit tests and 540 Deno tests

- 💰 **[FinAI](https://github.com/FinaiWorld)**: a multi-agent finance assistant. Specialized agents read live Plaid transactions to track spending, predict budgets and recommend actions, with LLM-summarized market news for context.
- 🧠 **[Knowledge Distillation for Efficient Vision Models](https://github.com/Abhinaykotla/Knowledge_Distilation_for_Smaller_Models_in_CV)**: compressed a 669 MB teacher CNN into a 6.5 MB student (99% smaller) at 97% accuracy using pruning and quantization.
